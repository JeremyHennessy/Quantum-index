# Phase 4 negative and uncertainty register (append-only)

1. **REJECT raw 6j-factor product without phases.** Example higher-spin left-face matrix entry: naive product predicts `-sqrt(2/3)`, while two independent CG/Haar methods agree on `+sqrt(2/3)`. This is a basis/vertex-phase error, not new physics; see `test_09`.
2. **REJECT using 8 quaternion axes for quartic Haar polynomials.** The axes produce E[q0^4]=1/4 versus Haar 1/8. The 24-cell is qualified only up to polynomial degree five per variable; no unsupported higher-spin integration.
3. **REJECT automatic volume-uniformity of crude energy-weighted global bound.** At alpha=1,beta=1/2,z=0 and J=1/2, exact bound rises from 5/24 for two plaquettes to 13/32 for three. Local Casimir penalties do not by themselves remove extensive magnetic terms.
4. **REJECT extrapolating this certificate through stronger magnetic continuum-motivated couplings.** At alpha=1,beta=1 (source a=1,g²=2) the three-plaquette cutoff J=1/2 denominator is negative. This says nothing about true spectral gap and instead blocks use of this proof method at those parameters.
5. **RETAIN as OPEN** the possibility of a gauge-invariant, local or conditionally localized energy-weighted estimate which does not require extensive global magnetic norms. No proof or empirical verification exists yet.
6. **KEEP Phase 3 counterexample:** for every cutoff J the raw high-spin coupling does not vanish. The present work does not revoke or conceal that negative result.

All numerical matrix gap values are finite-cutoff observations only. No novelty or four-dimensional physics claim is made.
