# Phase 11 — continuum relevance and research direction decision

**Decision for the *Clay objective*: PIVOT.** Preserve the fixed-strip work as a valid restricted strong-electric program. Do not automatically create Phase 12 or continue increasing finite-lattice perturbative order without a justified path to four-dimensional continuum Yang–Mills.

## Exactly what Phase 11 changes

- Finite volume: it proves a correct gauge-invariant two-level auxiliary-probe energy-coefficient identity and an independent local source-energy derivative, including complex left/right eigenvectors and vacuum-pole centering.
- In real strong-electric regime: the **min–max principle** gives the local energy shifts |D_N(z,lambda)|<=2|lambda| and |L_N(z,h)|<=2|h|, uniform in plaquette count, where the analytic branches coincide with the real ground states.
- **Still open:** a uniform nonselfadjoint complex (z,lambda,h) polydisk and bounded local defects; the full centered resolvent F_N(s,z) all-orders absolute coefficient tail; a continuum theorem.

These are legitimate finite-lattice conceptual tools; the probe identity itself is standard analytic second-order perturbation theory and is NOT a new physical mass-gap mechanism.

## Physical limiting regime is the decisive obstruction

The one-strip SU(2) Hamiltonian is a spatial two-dimensional plaquette chain, *not* the 3+1-dimensional Yang–Mills theory required by Clay. The reviewed (2+1)D convention
  alpha=g²/2, beta=2/(a²g²),
with dimensionful g², gives beta/alpha=4/[a²(g²)²]. At fixed positive g², a→0 makes beta/alpha arbitrarily large. The existing weak-magnetic/strong-electric cluster and Lie–Schwinger smallness hypotheses are therefore NOT uniform under that continuum scaling. Even a proof of the exact Phase 11 complex local-defect lemma at sufficiently small beta/alpha would be a restricted fixed-spacing theorem, with no demonstrated persistence toward the continuum.

In 3+1D the gauge coupling is dimensionless, the lattice coupling runs, and a quantitative ultraviolet-to-infrared renormalization framework is essential. Our present spatial strip changes both dimension and topology. Neither a uniform gap of a finite strip nor nonperturbative strong-electric local analyticity is a proof of an OS/Wightman continuum theory in R⁴, reflection positivity plus correct limiting Schwinger functions, nontriviality, or a physical positive mass gap for **every compact simple gauge group**.

## External source checks and modest next research direction

- Official Jaffe–Witten/Clay statement: https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf , §§4–6. Demands nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills on R⁴ satisfying strong axiomatic properties and a positive mass gap for every compact simple group.
- Michael R. Douglas, *The Yang–Mills Millennium Problem*, Nature Reviews Physics 8, 86–97 (published January 12, 2026), https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-025-00909-2 . Its reviewed high-level survey emphasizes nontrivial continuum existence/renormalization and the lack of a known complete route to the positive mass gap. This review is contextual, not a theorem.
- Phase 8/Phase 9 source-qualified SU(2) plaquette chain Hamiltonian: Ebner et al., Phys. Rev. D 110, 014505 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.014505 ; Communications Physics (2025) https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-025-02298-5 .
- Strict source distinction: existing mathematically controlled strong-coupling 2+1D/Euclidean mechanisms are not automatically equivalent to a 4D continuum construction; matching regulators, gauge-invariant observables, transfer/OS positivity, renormalized couplings and quantitative limits would require new proof.

**Recommended next investigation, rather than Phase 12:** Conduct a *source-only, sharply bounded theorem-obstruction audit* of constructive **four-dimensional** lattice Yang–Mills: choose an explicit reflection-positive 4D gauge-invariant Wilson lattice action and identify, using published rigorous renormalization group and continuum-limit work, the first unproved quantitative step needed for (a) nontrivial infinite-volume continuum Schwinger functions satisfying OS axioms, and separately (b) a volume-uniform positive transfer-matrix spectral gap in physical units. State the weak-coupling/asymptotically free scaling and all regulator, group and observable assumptions; do not write a proof before independently establishing the literature boundary.

This is a **pivot of the research question, not an achievement of constructive QFT**. The finite SU(2) local-probe result can be retained as a mathematical control/example but should not be mistaken for a shortcut to the Clay problem.
