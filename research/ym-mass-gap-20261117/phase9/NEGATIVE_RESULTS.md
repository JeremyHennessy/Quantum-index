# Phase 9 — negative results, rejected transfers, and unresolved assumptions

Additive ledger. Do not edit or relabel earlier Phases 1–8 negative results, historical claims or the charter.

1. **REJECT: Phase 8 nonzero sixth-order coefficient proves uniform locality or a gap.** The exact c_(4,6)(1,1)=1504856360495713/3397652693362287360000 is a single finite-volume perturbative coefficient. It cannot prove a bound on all k, all N or any continuum model. Its nonzero sign also says nothing about positivity of every c_(N,k).
2. **REJECT: low-spin projected sign can stand in for the physical coefficient.** The existing j<=1/2 sixth-order diagnostic is negative; the full perturbatively complete Phase 8 result positive. Both exact values remain retained. Higher-order truncation convergence is unproved.
3. **REJECT: extrapolate the volume-uniform ground-state *expectation* bound to the centered resolvent.** Henheik–Teufel–Wessel (2022), Theorem 3, applies to observables supported on a bounded local tensor-factor set. (H−E0+s)^(-1) is global and depends on the entire Hamiltonian; its sandwiched matrix element is not automatically a local operator. This is a **failed inference**, not a counterexample to the underlying conjectured resolvent locality.
4. **REJECT: real-beta uniform interacting gap by itself proves a uniform complex-beta analytic disk.** The published weak-interaction theorem supplies the real strong-electric gap; complex-beta H is generally non-selfadjoint and its Riesz projector/resolvent norms require additional control. A real-axis lower spectral bound is insufficient.
5. **REJECT: use Osterwalder–Seiler's Euclidean strong-coupling result as a theorem for the exact Hamiltonian G.** The accessible source states Euclidean positivity, infinite-volume analyticity and confinement. We do not possess a checked transfer-matrix correspondence or its detailed coupling normalizations to the specified continuous-time ladder-centered resolvent. Publisher access to the original full article was blocked, so the abstract was not silently promoted to an exact theorem map.
6. **REJECT: treat the fully Gauss-constrained physical Hilbert as a tensor product of individual plaquette sites.** The Gauss singlet constraints on shared links prevent such a factorization. The known weakly interacting theorem is instead applied first to the **unconstrained original-link tensor product**, where the unperturbed product vacuum and local interactions are rigorous. Only then is its unique ground and spectral lower bound restricted to the reducing Gauss-invariant sector.
7. **HOLD: explicit numerical strong-electric threshold and gap magnitude.** The cited stability and LPPL theorems supply existence of smallness thresholds and positive constants, not the numerical c_*, gamma_* needed to make a practical beta cutoff in the present normalization. Do not invent one, or represent a few finite-volume numerical gaps as that bound.
8. **HOLD: uniform absolute Taylor-coefficient bound.** The elementary sufficient finite-volume analytic radius ~1/N becomes small, but this is a *failure of the crude Kato norm estimate*, **not** a counterexample to uniform local analyticity. The weakest unresolved step is a common complex-coupling disk and N-independent bound for the physical centered resolvent, or an independently proved equivalent connected-cluster majorant.
9. **HOLD: finite-a thermodynamic limit is not continuum scaling.** Published weak-interaction results apply to fixed spatial lattice spacing and small |beta|/alpha. The (2+1)D convention beta/alpha=4/[a²(g²)²] grows as a→0 at fixed g². There is no justified passage to the 3+1D all-compact-simple-group Clay statement.
10. **NO NOVELTY CLAIM for the recovered theorem.** Yarotsky and Henheik–Teufel–Wessel established the underlying weak-interaction locality/gap statements long before this research. The 13-link geometric encoding is a straightforward local mapping, not an original physical mechanism.

**Qualified positive result retained:** published weak-interaction gap and LPPL Theorem 3 apply to the full original-link Hamiltonian and transfer its ground and spectral gap to the Gauss sector for sufficiently small |beta|/alpha. For any bounded local first-face observable, last-face ground-state influence decreases exponentially in N with a published unspecified rate. For real beta the physical centered G_N obeys 0<=G_N<=4/(s+gamma_*), but neither fact establishes a bound on G_N−G_(N−1) or the *absolute* coefficient tail.

**Decision:** B for recovery/application of published fixed-lattice strong-electric gap and equal-time expectation locality; D for the specific physical connected-resolvent coefficient tail. The smallest decisive next test is fully specified in ONE_PRECISE_OPEN_LEMMA.md.
