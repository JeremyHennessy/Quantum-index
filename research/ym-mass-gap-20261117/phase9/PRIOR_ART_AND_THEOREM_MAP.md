## The theorem correspondence is explicit but not a novelty claim

The key independently audited bridge is from the product of original SU(2) **link Hilbert spaces** (on which the strong-coupling theorems' product-vacuum hypotheses are true) to the **invariant gauge sector** (which reduces H). Neither the physical Hilbert space nor all its Gauss constraints are mistakenly treated as a tensor product of independent plaquette Hilbert spaces. The full unrestricted link system has a UNIQUE ground under the published weak-interaction smallness hypothesis; this ground must transform in a 1D representation of local gauge SU(2) groups. Those have no nontrivial 1D characters, so this ground lies in the physical sector. The physical excitation spectrum is contained in the full excitation spectrum; a full-system uniform gap therefore bounds the physical sector.

This is an **application/reproduction of established weak-interaction mathematics**. It does not independently reprove Yarotsky or Henheik–Teufel–Wessel, supply a numerical c_*, or construct G_N holomorphy. Full external peer review and exhaustive prior-art novelty research have not occurred. The mathematical question that survives is the additional *local dynamical resolvent* or equivalent uniform complex-beta coefficient majorant, for the centered response as actually defined.
