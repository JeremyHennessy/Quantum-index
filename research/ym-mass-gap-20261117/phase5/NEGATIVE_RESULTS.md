# Phase 5 negative and uncertainty register (research-only, append-only)

1. **Rejected: one-step locality implies full-resolvent locality.** For the gauge-invariant `η_N=χ_1(G₁)` and the full `Q_(j≤1/2)` sector, an added distant plaquette changes the exact coefficient of β² in the unsubtracted complementary resolvent by `1/[(8α−z)²(11α−z)]`, equal to `1/704` at α=1,z=0. The local one-step electric-weighted term is unchanged, but that is insufficient for the full resolvent. The difference is due to a disconnected, remote loop state, not proof of physical long-range propagation.
2. **Rejected: the 8-axis quaternion rule is generally accurate for spin-one state norms.** It gives `∫χ_1²≈3` rather than the Haar value `1`; degree-four moments require the qualified 24-cell or independent character orthogonality. The eight-axis rule remains exact for complete `j≤1/2` Gram/Wilson entries because those are degree ≤3 in each quaternion.
3. **Hold: full `jmax=1` four-plaquette magnetic Hamiltonian.** The full gauge-singlet basis **is** enumerated (233 states), but its complete magnetic matrices are **not** independently verified. No jmax=1 spectral-gap claim is admitted.
4. **Hold: finite-β / physical-scaling extrapolation.** The exact remote-loop perturbative result holds within a rigorously stated positive-resolvent neighbourhood of β=0. It does not establish the sign or magnitude of a finite-β volume difference, a continuum limit or a physical mass gap. The sufficient Neumann radius deteriorates as plaquette count increases.
5. **Hold: literature novelty.** Gauge spin networks, resolvent expansions and linked-cluster subtraction are established ideas. No Phase 5 result is certified as new in the research literature.
6. **Retain earlier failures without replacement.** Phase 3 raw high-spin tail suppression was disproved; Phase 4 the naïve global energy-weighted estimate worsened with spatial volume and failed adverse coupling tests. Those remain in the prior research registry and are not silently relabelled.

**Next single falsifier:** explicitly define a connected, vacuum-subtracted local response, verify it cancels the disconnected order-β² term *without assuming the full spectral gap*, then test the first coefficient at which a distant added plaquette can genuinely enter through a connected interaction path.
