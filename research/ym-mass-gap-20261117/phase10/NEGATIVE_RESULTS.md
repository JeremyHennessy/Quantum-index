# Phase 10 — negative-results and unresolved evidence register

Append-only Phase 10 source. Previous Phase 1–9 negative registers, charter and claim entries are immutable. There is no new four-dimensional continuum Yang–Mills proof.

1. **FALSIFIED specific inference:** "A common complex-coupling analytic isolated eigenbranch with an N-uniform spectral separation forces an N-uniform norm for the full Riesz projector or shifted resolvent." N independent decoupled two-level systems with h(z)=[[0,−z], [−z,1]] have eigenvalue spacing r(z)=sqrt(1+4z²) in a common disk, but at z=i/4 their full rank-one Riesz projector norms equal (2/sqrt(3))^N and diverge. A 2×2 triangular nonnormal matrix also has fixed spectrum {0,1} and arbitrarily large inverse norm. These counterexamples test the **shortcut**, not the exact SU(2) F_N conjecture.
2. **REJECT: theorem about uniform eigenvalue analyticity equals uniform centered dynamic susceptibility.** Del Vecchio et al. (JFA 2020) Theorems 3.6, 3.8 control the complex ground-energy eigenbranch and isolate the spectrum, and local block inverses appear in their proof. No N-uniform bound on the final global Riesz projection, shifted resolvent with arbitrary s>0 or the nonlocal centered F_N trace was established by the reviewed theorem statement. This is a non-applicability finding for the **full** Phase 9 lemma, not a counterexample to the theorem.
3. **REJECT: real Laplace identity automatically persists under complex coupling.** The spectral-theorem identity requires self-adjoint K>=0. At complex z the Hamiltonian is generally non-self-adjoint, the ground projection nonorthogonal, and the naive conjugate-normalized ground expression is not holomorphic. A simple finite-N semigroup norm bound grows as exp(2N|z|t), so it supplies no N-uniform argument. The correct analytic trace formula with P_N(z) is retained.
4. **REJECT: Yarotsky 2006 weak-* local analyticity already includes dynamical resolvents.** The original Theorem 1(4) states analyticity of the infinite-volume ground state's expectation of a **bounded local observable**; the centered resolvent involves the global inverse and frequency integration. Time-inserted polymer control requires an additional proved estimate.
5. **REJECT: Henheik–Teufel–Wessel 2022 Theorem 3 controls complex F_N.** The theorem bounds perturbations of ordinary bounded **local ground expectations** at *real self-adjoint* couplings. Its source locality and gap hypotheses are valid in Phase 9's strong-electric mapping, but the global resolvent is not a local A.
6. **RETAIN: product-projector blow-up does not disprove local response locality.** In the very same decoupled two-level countermodel, the fully centered local F_N(s,z)=1/[r(z)²(s+r(z))] is independent of N. This is a constructive countercontrol against erroneously treating a failed global-norm proof technique as falsification of the physical coefficient-tail conjecture.
7. **HOLD: full 2004 and 2005 Yarotsky proof-level inspection.** Primary full articles were not accessible. The 2006 primary theorem/proof and 2022 theorem/restatement were examined, and the 2020 Lie–Schwinger proof was available. Source access limitations remain explicit.
8. **HOLD: global physical complex-shifted inverse for every fixed s>0.** Complex eigenvalue isolation in a disk about E_N does not by itself certify exclusion of E_N−s or a useful resolvent norm bound, particularly for s exceeding the excluded spectral-radius scale. No uniform inverse norm assertion is made here.
9. **HOLD: N-uniform complex local dynamical scalar or linked coefficient tail.** Existence of a nontrivial disk r(alpha,s)>0 with sup_N |F_N(s,z)|<=M or an equivalent absolute connected-coefficient majorant remains unproved; no direct SU(2) counterexample was found. The next useful question is a uniform local resolvent estimate (not full projection norm control).
10. **HOLD: physical scaling and Clay.** The source-qualified (2+1)D strong-electric ratio beta/alpha diverges under fixed-g² continuum lattice-spacing decrease. A fixed-strong-electric theorem for the one-strip SU(2) model is not the four-dimensional continuum existence and mass gap for every compact simple group.
11. **NO ORIGINALITY CLAIM.** The real spectral Laplace identity is elementary functional calculus; the uniform complex eigenbranch theorem is published 2020 work, not ours; the two-level counterexample is a restricted verification of an invalid inference, not a new SU(2) physical discovery.

**Scientific decision:** B for the established source-qualified complex eigenbranch mapping and elementary real Laplace bridge, C for the particular nonnormal norm inference, D for the exact physical complex-resolvent all-orders locality estimate. Preserve the Phase 8 positive full-spin sixth-order result and earlier low-spin sign reversal unchanged.
