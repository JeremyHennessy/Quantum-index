\[

This is strictly weaker than a gap and could imply polynomial imaginary-time decay for that one probe. It does not control a dense family, so it cannot prove the Clay spectral-gap claim by itself. **More importantly, none of the inspected 2026 source proofs supplies the necessary four-dimensional regulator-uniform estimate or a direct valid route to such constants.** No value for γ, no renormalized test-function class and no Wilson-to-OS matching inequality has been sourced. Accordingly this is **NOT a selected research lemma**.
