## Pre-2026 rigorous work, precise scope

**T. Bałaban.** *Renormalization group approach to lattice gauge field theories. I*, Commun. Math. Phys. 109 (1987), 249–301, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01215223: **small-field approximation**, local effective actions and recursive coupling renormalization. *II Cluster expansions*, CMP116 (1988), 1–22, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01239022: small-field cluster tools. *Large field renormalization I/II*, CMP122 (1989), 175–202/355–392, DOIs https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01257412 , https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01238433: major large-field bounds; **Theorem 1 in the latter is conditional on the effective couplings remaining in a sufficiently small interval**. Its introductory discussion called a related supporting argument unpublished *at that time*; subsequent final publication of that exact inequality was not verified by this audit. Source access: original introduction transcribed in SciSpace, journal metadata; **not a complete independent verification of every RG proof**. The Jaffe–Witten statement §6 explicitly recognizes the qualitative advance without saying it completes continuum theory.

**J. Magnen, V. Rivasseau, R. Sénéor.** *Construction of YM4 with an infrared cutoff*, CMP155 (1993), 325–383, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02097397. Author-uploaded paper text https://www.researchgate.net/publication/251358884_Construction_of_YM_4_with_an_infrared_cutoff (author upload, line-segmented), abstract and introduction: builds rigorous basis of **pure SU2**, *trivial topological sector*, regularized **axial gauge with a momentum cutoff**, UV removal in a **fixed IR cutoff** setup, restores gauge Slavnov identities. The authors expressly say the infrared cutoff is kept and that they **do not provide every convergence argument in full detail** in that article. No regulator-free R4 OS gap theorem follows.

**Osterwalder–Seiler (1978).** Finite-lattice reflection positivity and a positive transfer matrix plus fixed-spacing *strong-coupling* infinite-volume analyticity and confinement bounds; abstract accessible, full technical theorem not independently inspected. At physical 4D asymptotically free a→0 the inverse bare coupling grows, so weak-bare-coupling UV estimates require a distinct proof; no transplant of a strong-coupling radius.

**Modern dimension-specific work.** Pure 2D/3D Yang–Mills stochastic or lattice results and 2026 massive *Yang–Mills–Higgs* (extra scalar matter) are not direct pure four-dimensional UV existence or gap theorems. Douglas, *Nature Reviews Physics* 8 (2026) 86–97, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-025-00909-2 (published Jan 12, 2026), predates the October formal submission; it records RG/continuum and gap as major open issues *as of its publication*.

## Scientific decision

A validated finite Wilson model + OS reconstruction + **qualitative** mixing is insufficient to prove mass gap. One **quantitative** estimate on **a dense family of centered gauge-invariant vectors** with a common exponential decay rate in *physical Euclidean time* suffices to supply M1 **once** W3–W5 and positive-energy OS representation are independently accepted. See ONE_OPEN_QUANTITATIVE_LEMMA.md for the precise quantifiers and a rigorous counterexample falsifying the qualitative shortcut. Do not portray the remaining gap estimate as automatic or proveable by a Wilson-loop area law alone.
