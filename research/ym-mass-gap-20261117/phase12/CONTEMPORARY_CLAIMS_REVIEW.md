## Claim 2 — Faizal and Shabir published April 20, 2026, mass gap + confinement

**Publisher primary:** M. Faizal and A. Shabir, *Reflection-Positive Construction of a Four-Dimensional SU(N) Yang–Mills Theory With Mass Gap and Confinement*, *Fortschritte der Physik* vol.74 issue4, article e70097, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/prop.70097 ; publisher reports **first published 2026-04-20**. arXiv abstract https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19362 dated **June 9, 2026** (later arXiv listing).

**Their own abstract claims:** reflection-positive regulated SU(N), a transfer operator with an N-independent gap, strong-coupling Wilson area law via character/polymer expansion, multi-scale inequalities uniformly carrying clustering/string tension to continuum, universality of regulators via telescoping Lipschitz bounds and matching weak-bare-coupling/asymptotic-freedom trajectory.

**CRITICAL INDEPENDENT REVIEW STATUS:** The Wiley page accessible in this audit returned the **abstract, bibliographic data and reference list** but not its technical theorem statements/proofs; the arXiv abstract was accessible but the full article proof could not be retrieved, and an identified institutional author upload appears embargoed into 2027. Thus the key coupling-/regulator-/volume-uniform estimates, the reflection-positive transfer-to-continuum correspondence and the claimed strong-to-weak identification were **not independently inspected or proved**. In particular the words “interlaced inequalities” and “universal” in an abstract are not substitutes for the required quantifiers, constants, error summability and physical scale. I found no authoritative outside acceptance of the entire Clay result. **Do not classify the theorem as disproved either**: this is **D — primary proof access / independent verification insufficient**, though the research claim is clearly published.

## Verifier decision vs physical proof

A Lean proof of a well-defined proposition A in imported axioms is decisive **for A** if checked soundly. It does not prove a stronger proposition B (such as mass-gap) absent a formal implication A→B. The group reviewers explicitly strengthened the original public target to a common-family combined acceptance, a meaningful defence against trivial disconnected witnesses; **that still does not insert a physical gap into the accepted proposition**. Equally, an inaccessible published proof of B cannot be called false merely because outsiders have not accepted it. Keep **verifier success**, **semantic/physical assessment**, **source access** and **community recognition** in separate columns of subsequent reviews.

**Phase 12 conclusion:** For an audit of the *strongest accessible group-assessed existence chain*, the next explicit missing spectral input is a uniform physical time-exponential estimate on a dense family of renormalized gauge-invariant states in the **same** selected OS component. This is specified (but not proved) in ONE_OPEN_QUANTITATIVE_LEMMA.md. The whole Clay problem remains open.
