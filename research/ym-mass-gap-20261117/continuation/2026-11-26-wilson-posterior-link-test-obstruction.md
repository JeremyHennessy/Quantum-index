# A large-bare-coupling obstruction to the posterior single-link test

**2026-11-26 UTC. One step of the actual SU(2) kernel; bounded local tilt regime. An obstruction to a proof method, not a statement that correlations fail to decay.**

The [positive seed representation](2026-11-26-wilson-positive-seed-posterior.md) gives a sufficient single-site comparison bound. Its numerical failure at large bare coupling is not itself a mathematical obstruction. Here the actual worst-case one-fine-link conditional coefficients of that joint representation can be shown to approach one, for six other fine links in a row, uniformly in the observed output. This holds with fixed patch and d=o(beta_W), where d is the log oscillation bound of a blocking noise factor. More generally the proof below explicitly states the required scale separation.

## Construct distinct modes at a common boundary

Use [the pinned Wilson action](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Wilson/Law.lean#L29-L56) and [defining-representation price](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Gauge/MatrixGroup.lean#L112-L144). Identify SU(2) with unit quaternions; this price is 1 minus the real quaternion coordinate. The conditional weight for fine bond i is proportional to exp(beta_W dot(u,S)), where S is the sum of the six oriented staples (or their inverses depending on which convention names S).

On a fine torus of side >=3, the six plaquettes incident to i have six distinct parallel opposite bonds j, at transverse offsets ±unit in the three other axes. Each opposite bond occurs in just its own plaquette among these six. Set the transverse links to identity, and set five opposite bonds so their staple sum is 1 (three +1 and two −1). Select the sixth to give first +1, then a fixed pure unit quaternion w (choose its inverse if the naming convention requires). The two exteriors differ only in j. The staple vectors are S_0=2 and S_1=1+w, with normalized modes at 1 and (1+w)/sqrt(2). The argument works for each of the six j, separately in the supremum defining that coefficient. The six distinct opposite indices were checked for all four bond directions on sides 3 through 10 (192 indices); their distinctness for all sides >=3 follows directly from the offsets.

## The bounded posterior tilt cannot hide both modes

In the seed posterior hold all seeds fixed and choose any coarse output V. Each fine link participates in at most D coarse likelihood factors, with D from the preceding note. Their log oscillation in u is at most Dd, regardless of the nonsmooth tag selection. Thus changing the fine-link conditional from bare Wilson to posterior multiplies an upper bound on any event probability by at most exp(Dd). This is true for either of the two exteriors even if the noise factors themselves change.

Let r be any positive chord radius less than half the Euclidean quaternion distance between the two modes. Here use the ordinary Euclidean norm of a unit quaternion difference. For the Gibbs density exp(beta_W dot(u,S_k)), outside its radius-r cap the unit dot product is at most 1−r²/2, while within radius r/2 it is at least 1−r²/8. Haar measure of the latter cap is a common m_r>0. Each posterior puts mass at least 1−m_r^(−1) exp(Dd−c beta_W) in its own radius-r cap, for c=3 sqrt(2) r²/8. The caps are disjoint. Consequently every one of the six one-coordinate influences satisfies
`c_ij ≥ 1−2 exp(Dd−c beta_W)/m_r`.
All fixed-output conditionals used here are specified directly by the everywhere positive joint density in the seed representation. If instead an essential exterior supremum is used, perturb the finitely many neighboring fine links in small open product neighborhoods: the staple modes remain separated and both norms stay positive. The bound then holds with a slightly smaller c, because the tilt oscillation bound does not require continuity.

For fixed patch, if Dd−c beta_W tends to −infinity the row has limiting lower bound 6, not merely a failed upper estimate. This includes d=o(beta_W). The estimate does not say that this scale condition holds on the pinned trajectory, and it does not exclude another coupling, gauge quotient, block comparison, or an averaged criterion. It explicitly separates a worst-case conditional-link obstruction from actual decay of trace correlations. The next target is a gauge-invariant or averaged block method that does not require contraction at these forced exteriors.
