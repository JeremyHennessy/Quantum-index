# A free blocking control where separated source transport is exactly local

**2026-11-26 UTC. Free Gaussian calibration only.** This is the complementary test to the [false mean-only signal](2026-11-26-third-order-blocking-source-jets.md). Finite-range Gaussian decompositions are established methods; see [Brydges–Guadagni–Mitter](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0303013) and [Bauerschmidt](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2212).

## A positive split and its actual stochastic kernel

Let C be the free Maxwell curvature covariance, and let ρ be a radial nonnegative probability density supported in radius h. Put Q=(ρ*ρ̃)*, acting as the scalar multiplier |ρ̂|² on each curvature component, with ρ̃(x)=ρ(−x). Then 0≤Q≤I and Q commutes with C. Independent Gaussian fields R and E may be realized with covariances QC and (I−Q)C. This realizes the full field F=R+E in distribution. Equivalently there is an explicit fine-to-retained conditional kernel: given F, draw independent centered Gaussian ξ of covariance Q(I−Q)C and set R=QF+ξ. Then Cov(R)=QC and Cov(F−R,R)=0. Joint Gaussianity makes the residual independent of R. This conditional kernel is neither the deterministic smoothing of the same sample nor a nonlinear Wilson block. For a finite field where the deterministic smoothing multiplier is nonzero on every supported mode, observing the exact smoothed field in fact determines F, so its conditional eliminated variance is zero. For a deterministic multiplier with zeros the Gaussian conditioning leaves only the corresponding unobserved modes, not the graded positive split (I−Q)C.

The components of C are second derivatives of the scalar Green function and harmonic away from the diagonal. This includes the zero-mode-removed flat periodic case: the off-diagonal scalar Laplacian is constant and its derivatives vanish. Radial averaging thus leaves C(x−y) exactly unchanged for torus or Euclidean distance >2h, with the averaging ball inside a flat chart in the torus case. A preliminary convolution smoothing of the field by a probability mollifier supported in radius δ gives the conservative condition >2(h+δ), and makes Wick squares ordinary smooth Gaussian observables. Across supports at these strict pair distances the eliminated Gaussian restrictions are mutually independent. Conditional on R, any integrable insertion that uses only the field in its own support has conditionally factorized joint raw moments (assuming the relevant integrability). Thus every joint moment and joint cumulant of those distinct packet insertions is exactly reproduced by their retained conditional means, for any finite number of mutually separated packets. For a centered quadratic packet that mean is the retained centered Wick-square packet. This preserves any existing finite-box signal; it does not assert that the torus or lattice triplet must vanish.

## Exact periodic lattice version

Write P for nearest-neighbour averaging, G=(I−P)⁻¹ on mean-zero inputs, and C=d(G⊗I)d* for the curvature covariance up to its common constant. Translation-invariant P commutes with the differences. For Q=P^(2m), both split covariances are positive since the spectrum of P lies in [−1,1]. On the mean-zero subspace, the eliminated covariance is exactly
`d[(I+P+⋯+P^(2m−1))⊗I]d*`.
The projection onto constants is killed by d. Walk support has graph radius (2m−1)a and two coordinate differences expand it by at most 2a. Separation greater than (2m+1)a gives exact independence between eliminated packet restrictions. The polynomial identity and nonnegative split/noise multipliers were independently checked in exact integer arithmetic at 136 rational eigenvalue/order pairs (p=j/8, −8≤j≤8, 1≤m≤8). The proof is independent of this check.

For fixed physical range as a→0, let ρ be the stopped nearest-neighbour walk's exit law from an origin-centered symmetric lattice ball of radius h, and g its expected pre-exit occupation. Telescoping against test functions gives δ₀−ρ=(I−P)g. As P is symmetric, δ₀−ρ*ρ̃=(I−P)(g+ρ*g̃). The eliminated curvature kernel is consequently finite range, with conservative support bound 2(h+a)+2a, provided the corresponding ball and support comparisons are made in graph distance (or consistent lattice distances with a one-step bound). Its positive multipliers follow exactly as for radial averaging; no rotational invariance of the lattice exit law is needed. The identity is analytic; the fixed-step arithmetic checks do not test exit distributions.

**Interpretation.** Run both Gaussian cases in any cubic source calculation: for the earlier split the signed omitted terms must cancel the apparent loop; for this split sufficiently separated omitted terms are individually zero. A calculation that passes these controls still has to handle the actual Wilson conditional law and prove its signed physical-log estimate. No Lean replay, Wilson matching, or mass-gap claim.
