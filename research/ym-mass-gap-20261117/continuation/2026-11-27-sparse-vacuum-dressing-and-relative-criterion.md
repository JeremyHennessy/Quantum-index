# Vacuum dressing repairs the sparse blocking control

**2026-11-27 UTC. Fixed spatial regulator.** This note gives a positive control for the [bare-projection failure](2026-11-27-straight-blocking-light-band-and-vacuum-leakage.md), using the same SU(2) model, and a sufficient local criterion for a possible overlapping-interaction extension.

## Exact disjoint-face dressing

In the sparse model put H_p=A_p−g_p W_p on each selected disjoint four-link face. The remaining links are free. Write ψ=a·1+sη for the normalized positive local vacuum, a>0, η⊥1 normalized and s=√(1−a²). Positivity of the elliptic semigroup and gauge invariance of H_p imply uniqueness and gauge invariance of ψ. Take U_p to be the rotation sending 1 to ψ and η to −s·1+aη, identity off this plane, and identity if s=0. The two vectors in the plane are gauge singlets at all corners, so this defines an ambient gauge-equivariant unitary, including on charged link sectors. It preserves the electric operator domain because both rotation vectors lie in that domain.

The sharper electric norm follows by projecting the eigenvalue equation orthogonally to the *constant*. Let Q_0=I−|1⟩⟨1|. Since E_p≤0 (the constant trial has energy zero), (A_p−E_p)Q_0ψ=g_p Q_0W_pψ. The operator A_p(A_p−E_p)⁻¹ on Q_0 has norm at most one. At E_p=0 interpret the inverse only on Q_0. Hence ||A_pψ||≤|g_p|. No estimate of ⟨A_p⟩ is required.

On the rotation plane the row creating η in U_p−I has norm √(s²+(a−1)²)=√(2(1−a)). Thus

    ||A_p(U_p−I)|| = √(2/(1+a)) ||A_pψ|| ≤ √2 |g_p|,

with the same estimate for U_p*−I. Expanding U_p* A_p U_p−A_p with one U_p factor kept unitary gives electric difference norm ≤2√2 |g_p| (the second bounded term is an adjoint). Since |E_p|≤|g_p|, the total local error B_p=U_p*(H_p−E_p)U_p−A_p satisfies ||B_p||≤(2√2+2)|g_p|<5|g_p|. It kills the constant exactly. On the ambient link tensor space, A_p≥(3/2)Q_0; it is essential not to use the larger singlet gap for a local block carrying boundary charges. Therefore |⟨x,B_px⟩|≤(10/3)|g_p|⟨x,A_px⟩.

Tensor the rotations on disjoint blocks. They send the global constant to the exact vacuum and commute with the physical gauge projection. The errors add without a volume factor:

    (1−ε)A ≤ U*(H−E_0)U ≤ (1+ε)A,   ε=(10/3) sup_p |g_p|.

At sup |g_p|≤1/400 this yields ε≤1/120. For the dressed retained space U(S⊕B), the discarded floor is at least 9·119/120=8.925. By min–max the light physical band, counting exactly its free multiplicity, lies in [5.95,6.05]; it has the vacuum below and all remaining levels at 8.925 or above. The bare discarded floor for this same sequence can approach zero without any physical gap closing. The same argument works for disjoint link blocks with bounded zero-Haar-mean gauge-invariant potentials, using the maximum block potential norm.

## A sufficient connected-volume estimate for a dressed interacting model

Here is a separate elementary criterion that converts vacuum cancellation of a bounded interaction into electric relative control. It need not assume the individual starting interaction terms already annihilate the vacuum. It is conditional when applied to the overlapping gauge model.

On a finite tensor product let Ω_0=⊗1 and A=Σ A_e with A_e≥δ(I−|1⟩⟨1|)_e, δ>0. Suppose B=B* equals Σ_X B_X with bounded self-adjoint terms supported on X, and BΩ_0=0. Write B_XΩ_X=b_{X,∅}Ω_X+Σ_{∅≠Y⊂X} b_{X,Y}⊗Ω_{X\Y} using the orthogonal decomposition by exact excited support. For each Y let C_{X,Y}=|b_{X,Y}⟩⟨Ω_Y| acting on Y and as identity elsewhere. Subtract the scalar b_{X,∅} and all C_{X,Y}+C_{X,Y}* from B_X. The resulting term D_X kills Ω_X. These subtractions sum to zero as operators: project BΩ_0 onto each exact excited support Y to get Σ_{X⊃Y} b_{X,Y}=0 and onto the vacuum to get Σ_X b_{X,∅}=0. For each fixed Y the creator and its adjoint are defined on exactly the same tensor factors, so this is cancellation of operators, not merely of their action on Ω_0.

Orthogonality and Cauchy–Schwarz give ||D_X||≤(2+2√(2^|X|−1)) ||B_X||. Put K=sup_e Σ_{X∋e}(2+2√(2^|X|−1))||B_X||. The support projections commute, hence I−|Ω_X⟩⟨Ω_X| ≤ Σ_{e∈X}(I−|1⟩⟨1|)_e. Summing the local relative bounds yields

    |⟨u,Bu⟩| ≤ (K/δ) ⟨u,Au⟩.

If a vacuum-dressed full Wilson Hamiltonian admitted such a decomposition of B=U*(H−E_0)U−A with sufficiently small uniform K, then min–max would give an isolated light band and a controlled discarded floor as above. This criterion does not prove bounded localization of the error generated by conjugating the unbounded electric operator. Nor does decay in the diameter of X as supplied by general quasi-locality alone imply the support-cardinality weighted bound. These are two concrete obligations, rather than consequences of transporting the vacuum.

## Literature and scope

Ground-state transport and relative perturbation methods are established; see [Yarotsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0412040), [Bachmann et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0842), and [Nachtergaele, Sims and Young](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02428) for the unbounded on-site setting. Gauge-equivariance of a spectral-flow generator built from H(s) and H'(s) follows if the uniform ambient gap and the locality hypotheses are verified. A ground-state flow does not by itself assert a uniform bound for the dressed electric error or a separation of the entire light band. The disjoint-face result here is a calibration of the prior blocking failure, not a priority claim or spatial continuum result.
