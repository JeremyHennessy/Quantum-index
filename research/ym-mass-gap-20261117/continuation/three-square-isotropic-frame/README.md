The same magnitude and signs in trace phases were independently replayed via exact recoupling. The diagonal entries and other first-order terms vanish by separate center flips. Parity and analyticity of the polar frame then make the end-pair amplitude divisible by b₂²b₃, while the perimeter amplitude is allowed at b₂b₃.
