Thus the lower test at 0 has zero kernel in these lines. Stratum j is the intersection of the kernels of the lower tests: the fresh strata at 1,2,3 vanish, and stratum 0 is all of Field 0.

[System.assemble](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/OPE/Orbits/System.lean#L15-L21) makes the scalar exactly λ₀(fresh₀(x;o;v)), equivalently λ₀(checkReal₀(x;o;v)) because knife₀ is the identity. It is a scalar of the constructed reader rather than a fitted projection.

## Exact transfer bound, and what centering changes

For K compact and separated, use the [public compact remainder](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/verification/CompactCheck.lean#L80-L93) with D=3, ε=1/2, no spectators and total weight 12. Uniformly on K and over the selected, unselected and other viewers, the same scalar approximates each triplet of the **same** labels with error at most r^(-17/2); the difference of two viewers is at most 2r^(-17/2). The normalized difference is bounded by 2r^(7/2) log^4(1/r), which vanishes. This is stronger than the grade-zero truncation alone because there are no fresh contributions at grades 1–3. The upstream raw-prescription vacuum_truncation and vacuum_error already supply the analogous grade-zero comparison; the stronger displayed rate uses the actual orbit-reader strata.

For a connected triplet obtained by **recentering separately** in each viewer, one must not apply the same-label inequality directly. At separated locations and for translation-invariant means m_σ, unit insertion and multilinearity give
`G₃,conn^σ = G₃,raw^σ − m_σ Σ_{i<j} G₂,ij,raw^σ + 2m_σ³.`
On the same fixed shapes use the pair OPE at D=0, ε=1/2: total weight 8, a finite common set of coefficients each bounded by r^(-17/2), and remainder at most r^(-15/2). The normalized pair subtraction therefore vanishes at O(r^(7/2) log^4(1/r)) for each fixed finite m_σ. In particular switching to independently centered labels for any two fixed viewers does not change the normalized limit. A uniform-in-viewer statement for this additional recentering would require a uniform bound on the means; the fixed-label estimate needs no such bound. The two-point asymptotic is not needed for this correction.
