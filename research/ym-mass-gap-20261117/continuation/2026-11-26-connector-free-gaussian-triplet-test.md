# A connector-free three-packet falsifier of squared-Gaussian transport

**2026-11-26 UTC. Exact bounded-model test, applicable to finite separated data without a coincident composite product. No assertion of a violation by Wilson data.**

The [connector obstruction](2026-11-26-connector-dependent-wick-obstruction.md) makes the transported flux cumulant unsuitable as a path-independent diagnostic. A different, one-sided diagnostic uses only two- and three-point statistics of local *scalar* composites. It asks whether their correlations could arise entirely from squaring jointly Gaussian fluctuations. It never selects a common color frame or subtracts an invented infrared baseline.

Let X_i, i=1,2,3, be jointly centered real Gaussian vectors, with arbitrary dimensions, and put A_i=||X_i||²−E||X_i||². Write C_ij=E[X_i X_j^T]. Wick pairing (or derivatives of the [quadratic-form generating function](https://www.ism.ac.jp/editsec/aism/pdf/044_4_0769.pdf)) gives g_ij=E[A_i A_j]=2||C_ij||_F² and h=E[A_1 A_2 A_3]=8 tr(C_12 C_23 C_31). Centering eliminates pairings internal to any one site. The eight triangle pairings give the factor eight. By trace Cauchy–Schwarz and operator norm bounded by Frobenius norm,

`g_ij ≥ 0,  |h|² ≤ 8 g_12 g_23 g_31.`

The constant is sharp without extra hypotheses: scalar Gaussian packets of nonzero pair correlations attain equality. Arbitrary random post-rotations of the underlying vectors, even if dependent on them, leave all four local squared-norm statistics untouched. This avoids the false remainders of the preceding note. All comparisons concern the squared-Gaussian model, not arbitrary Wick polynomials or signed quadratic forms. A common overall sign of the probes is harmless for this test.

At finite regulator, any nonnegative scalar smearing of a sum of squared Gaussian components is again a squared Gaussian norm: concatenate the component vectors after multiplying by the square roots of the test weights. This includes many transverse modes and arbitrary cross-packet correlations. Passing to finite separated limits of these four moments preserves the inequality by continuity; it does not require an a priori coincident-point L² observable. Hence for three disjoint nonnegative physical packets of the **actual matched trace**, one can test the hypothesis that its separated moments arise from centered, one-sign Gaussian quadratic composites (with possible regulator-dependent normalizations), using only the actual scalar two- and three-point data. A violation falsifies that class. A pass does not imply Gaussian behavior or a mass gap; massive and massless Gaussian controls can both pass. The construction's short-distance Wick match does not determine this test at physical separations.
