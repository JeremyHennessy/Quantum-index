## Polynomial realization of the actual transfer

Write x,y,z for the half-traces of U,V,UV⁻¹ in shared-root tree gauge. The two independent Haar SU(2) matrices are uniform unit quaternions. With negative Laplacian normalized to fundamental eigenvalue 3, the three commuting kinetic generators act on monomials as follows: on U use the pair (x,z) with remaining variable y; on V use (y,z) with remaining x; on the shared left multiplication use (x,y) with remaining z. For a pair (u,v) with remaining w, the positive generator is
```
3u ∂u + 3v ∂v − (1−u²)∂uu − (1−v²)∂vv − 2(w−uv)∂uv.
```
This follows by taking derivatives of the quaternion coordinates: Γ(u,v)=w−uv, Γ(u,u)=1−u², and Δu=−3u. In the shared case z is invariant under common left multiplication, because the trace of the resulting conjugate is unchanged. These operations give the Casimirs of the two outer paths and the common edge in the [physical spin-network basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9906036). On x^i y^j z^k the leading labels are (i+k,j+k,i+j), a bijection to admissible triples. The leading eigenvalue of the integer combination (1,1000,1000000) of generators is diagonal, and all remaining terms lower ordinary polynomial degree. The diagonal digits are distinct for labels used in the verifier. Back substitution produces the simultaneous eigenpolynomials without a numerical basis decomposition. K then acts by the true kinetic multipliers a_n³a_m³a_l from the preceding two-square note.

## Certification

Use M_p equal to degree-four Taylor of exp(b(x+y)/2); its operator error is at most 2b⁵/5! because |x+y|≤2. Keep K labels n+m≤5, enclosing each retained multiplier by positive-term Bessel intervals and rational rounding. Omitted eigenvalues are ≤4⁻¹⁸, since a_n≤4⁻ⁿ and n+m≥6. Construct S_p=M_p K_p M_p on polynomials exactly. The full-transfer operator error is bounded by 4(4⁻¹⁸+2×10⁻¹⁹)+8b⁵/5!; this uses norms <2 for both multipliers and ≤1 for K_p. There is no finite-volume sampling error.

For Haar inner products, condition on the real parts x and y. Then z=xy+√(1−x²)√(1−y²)t with t uniform on [−1,1], independently. Even moments of either real part are Catalan/4^n and odd moments vanish. Binomial expansion gives exact rational integrals, including ∫x²=1/4 and ∫xyz=1/16. Each trial vector has squared norm within 10⁻⁸ of 1. Bound the exact polynomial residual of S_p by the sum of absolute coefficients, since all coordinates have modulus ≤1; add the full-transfer operator error times the norm.

The residuals are both <6.49×10⁻¹¹. For the vacuum trial energy in (0.999,1.021), every other eigenvalue is at least 0.9 away. For the perimeter trial energy in (0.00018,0.00021), every other eigenvalue is at least 10⁻⁴ away: the two one-face states are bounded below by e^(−2b)(0.23)^4, and the fourth and later nonvacuum states are at most (50/49)(1/4)^6/24. These use the unnormalized min–max bounds from the preceding note, with the same isolated band continued for b between zero and 1/100. Spectral projection bounds the norm of each orthogonal error by residual divided by separation; norm adjustment and an allowed choice of sign cost at most an additional equal term plus the trial norm error. The exact trial transition is approximately −7.2356316543×10⁻⁵ in the selected trial phases; the possible error is ≤1.318×10⁻⁶. The certified magnitude therefore exceeds 7×10⁻⁵.
