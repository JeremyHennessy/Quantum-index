At every nonzero coarse momentum, the Gaussian vacuum has a uniformly gapped fiber and a bounded conditional response, but the coarse marginal remains massless. Harmonic zero modes are excluded explicitly.

Let the fine periodic spatial torus have even L, and block along edge-disjoint length-two axis paths starting at sites 2n. Linearized holonomy is the two-link sum. On a nonzero coarse Fourier momentum k∈[−π,π)^3, its eight aliases are q_b=k/2+πb modulo 2π, b∈{0,1}³. With unitary fine and coarse Fourier transforms the path map before transverse projection applies diag(1+e^(iq_i))/√8 to alias q. Divide the map by √2 to use orthonormal coarse coordinates, i.e., the horizontal metric of the prior note. Write A_b=P(k)diag(1+e^(iq_i))/4 and A=(A_b)_b, acting on the direct sum of fine transverse planes d(q_b)^⊥. Because (1+e^(iq_i))d_i(q)=d_i(k), A annihilates each fine longitudinal plane (the one-dimensional longitudinal direction). Thus inserting the fine transverse projections leaves AA* unchanged, and summing the eight squared multipliers gives AA*=I on the coarse transverse plane.
