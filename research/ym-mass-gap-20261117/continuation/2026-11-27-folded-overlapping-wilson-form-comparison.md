# Overlapping Wilson faces: a strong-electric spectral comparison via folded Lie–Schwinger dressing

**2026-11-27 UTC. Fixed spatial lattice, continuous time.** This is an application of [Del Vecchio, Fröhlich and Pizzo](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.13907) (JFA 284 (2023), 109734), not a new general block-diagonalization theorem. It extends the [sparse calibration](2026-11-27-sparse-vacuum-dressing-and-relative-criterion.md) to overlapping plaquettes at sufficiently small magnetic coupling, for a global quadratic-form comparison. The transformation is not shown local in the original periodic metric.

Let H=A−Σ_p g_pW_p on the spatial SU(2) torus, as in the [continuous-time limit note](2026-11-27-wilson-bridge-hamiltonian-limit.md), with all sides at least six, g*=sup_p |g_p| and real, possibly nonuniform g_p. For all sufficiently small g*, the construction below gives a gauge-equivariant U sending the electric vacuum to the interacting vacuum, with

    (1−ε)A ≤ U*(H−E_0)U ≤ (1+ε)A,   ε=O(√g*),

independent of the torus sizes. The threshold here is existential; it is not the explicit 1/400 of the sparse calibration, nor the previously proved gap-only window.

## Folded geometry and symmetry

Map each cyclic coordinate x to min(x,L−x) using its representative in {0,…,L−1}. Group the three outgoing link factors of every original vertex with its folded image. A cell covers at most eight original vertices; pad each site to 24 copies of L²(SU(2)), and the rectangular box to an open cube, with only free Casimirs on unused factors. The electric part is exactly the sum of the cell Hamiltonians, plus the added free terms. Under the 2/3 normalization each cell Hamiltonian has simple product constant vacuum and gap one. Each plaquette has anchors x, x+e_i and x+e_j, so its folded support sits in a rectangle of total side length at most two. An odd seam can give a one-cell plaquette; assign it to that cell's adjacent range-one rectangle (use the positive first-axis neighbor unless at the last folded coordinate, then the negative one). Each rectangle has at most four cells and thus at most 96 assigned plaquettes by the anchored-face count. An independent enumeration over sides 6 through 10 checks all 192,000 plaquettes on 125 tori: 480 one-cell cases, maximum fiber size eight, and maximum 16 faces per assigned rectangle with this assignment. The analytic bound of 96, not the observed 16, is used.

A site gauge transformation factors over the relabeled link Hilbert spaces; its restriction to a block preserves the constant, and the original electric and plaquette terms commute with it. The local projections, matrix blocks, reduced resolvents and commutators in the Lie–Schwinger recursion therefore commute with the gauge action by induction. Apply the same observation to exp(iφ Q_dummy) for each dummy link's vacuum-complement projection. The resulting unitary preserves the all-dummy-constant sector and the original physical Hilbert space. This symmetry check does not use locality of the gauge action in the folded metric.

## Range-two inputs in the weighted induction

Write the normalized perturbation as θΣ_J V_J with θ=√g* and A'=(2/3)A (g*=0 is immediate). Initial V_J vanish outside ranges one and two and satisfy ||V_J||≤64θ. In [DFP](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.13907#page=7), Section 2 expressly takes nearest-neighbor input for the displayed proof and says finite range can be treated by the method. It is not enough here to cite their final positive-gap statement: the application needs their terminal interaction norms with locally diagonal vacuum blocks.

* **Initialization and small labels.** With x_d=60 in d=3, 64θ is below both initial bounds (3.81), including θ^(1/3)/2^66 at r=2, once θ is sufficiently small. For a fixed target of r≤2 only steps on its subrectangles can contribute to its update. Each has at most four sites and only finitely many subrectangles, with a bound independent of total volume. Before its own diagonal step each change is a finite sum of commutator increments with weighted norm O(θ w_K w_L), using the local conjugation estimate (4.277)–(4.285) for targets K,L. At its own step the leading diagonal projection is bounded in the weighted norm (the vacuum projection commutes with the free energy) and the remainder is O(θ w_J²) by Lemma 4.1. The local gap before its own step is one for r=1, and at least 1−O(θ²) at r=2 from the previously processed r=1 terms, after centering. Hence the r≤2 norms remain O(θ) throughout. In particular they meet the r=2 inequalities at all regimes, not just at input.
* **R1 branches for r>2.** The label expansion in Definition 3.2 telescopes into nested updates and stops at a previously processed potential or a nonzero input. Its unmodified input term at the target is zero for r>2. Every nonzero branch therefore has at least two labels (including the leaf), with connected union and bounding rectangle the target. The new possibility is a size-two leaf, with the same inductive factor θ^((ρ−1)/3)/ρ^60 as any other ρ=2 rectangle. The product estimate (4.276) and its last step on p. 41 use this factor on **all** labels, including the leaf, and require only Σρ≥r and at least two labels. They do not use a size-one initial leaf. The rectangle-set injection and weighted walk count in (3.90)–(3.97), Appendix B, apply to those same label sets, with the leaf counted once. In the R1 subregime where the maximum update size is one, the leaf can have size two: the walk count then uses max(2,k) in place of k, also in the lower bound floor(c_d r/k) for the number of steps. This retains the polynomial-versus-stretched-exponential estimate uniformly in r. For the finitely many small r not covered by that asymptotic estimate the branch bound has an extra positive power of θ, which absorbs the fixed polynomial factors. The input already vanishes there for r>2.
* **R2, R3 and S2.** Starting with the R1 bound, these parts of Theorem 3.1 estimate updates by their effective norms and the reached local block diagonalizations; they have no further hypothesis on the original support length. The gap proof for the next subblock (Lemma 2.11 and Corollary 2.12) uses the already controlled centered forms, so the same bootstrap closes. The terminal output is therefore controlled as in (3.85), w_J≤96 θ^((r−1)/3)/r^60, with the local vacuum line preserved. This is a finite-range initialization check for this use of the cited induction, not an independent reproof of their general estimates.

## Extracting the stronger operator bound

For a final term V_J with local vacuum projection P_J, put Q_J=I−P_J and v_J=⟨Ω_J,V_JΩ_J⟩. In block form V_J−v_J I kills P_J and has no mixed entries. By definition of w_J and |v_J|≤w_J,

    |(V_J−v_J I)[u,u]| ≤ w_J⟨u,A'_J u⟩+2w_J||Q_J u||²
                         ≤ 3w_J⟨u,A'_J u⟩.

The last step uses A'_J≥Q_J. A site belongs to at most (r+1)^5 rectangles of a given r in three dimensions: there are at most (r+1)^2 triples of side lengths summing to r and at most (r+1)^3 translations containing that site for a fixed triple. Therefore ε=3θ sup_x Σ_(J∋x) w_J is O(θ) uniformly in the volume. The product vacuum is the simple ground state of the final form since ε<1 and the local shifts sum to its energy. Undo the scalar normalization; then restrict to physical gauge invariants with all dummy links held constant as above.

The electric plaquette reference energy is 6 and the next physical energy is 9. Min–max gives the whole interacting light band, with the reference multiplicity, in [6(1−ε),6(1+ε)] and all higher energies at least 9(1−ε). The same 9(1−ε) bounds the energy above E_0 on the dressed discarded subspace from the prior note. For ε<1/5 these bounds separate. This gives more low-energy spectral information than positivity of the gap alone.

## Boundary of the result

The folding groups some original distant sites in one cell. The estimate controls energies but cannot certify a physically local blocking map on the torus or a uniform bound along the weak-coupling continuum trajectory. The next test is to preserve the weighted induction with the periodic metric itself (e.g. wrap-aware minimal rectangles, with explicit treatment once supports wind), or to derive a different physically local form comparison.
