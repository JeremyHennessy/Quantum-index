# Local reference bound on the strict repeated-cosine surfaces

In the fixed-spacing separated reference model at E=−13/336, every momentum
point with exactly two equal cosines, all three in (−2,2), has a neighborhood
on which the localized reference resolvent matrix elements are bounded by
the established monotone Cauchy–Green argument. This is a local statement
at a surface; it neither asserts a global bound nor covers the physical
dressed boundary problem or continuum limit.

The proof combines the exact scalar curve, flat overlap, cross-elementary,
and same-elementary checks in this directory:

1. The elementary polynomial has simple real energies throughout the
   strict repeated chart. The flat summed energy can only come from the
   quadratic elementary factor; the flat threshold is a regular graph.
2. At a point with no threshold mode there is a local spectral gap. At a
   point with only the flat threshold, the isolated three-mode cluster
   has the nonzero scalar flat derivative. At a point with only a single
   residual threshold factor vanishing, smoothness of the scalar curve
   gives a nonzero derivative. In the absence of a flat threshold, a
   higher Hermitian nullity would force all determinant derivatives to
   vanish, so the residual mode in that case is simple.
3. At the six flat–residual overlaps, the exact slopes and multiplicity
   check give a definite derivative on the single full four-mode
   cluster, including both flat-rank jumps.
4. Off the flat graph, the fifteen cross-elementary pairs are transverse
   and have exactly two simple summed modes. The third elementary branch
   and any hidden same-branch multiplicity are excluded. The two
   independent gradients give a tangent with both derivatives positive.
5. Off the flat graph, simultaneous zeros of both factors within one
   elementary branch occur at exactly two strict representatives. There
   are no other threshold modes at either. The derivative of the full
   two-mode cluster in the repeated-surface tangent has determinant of
   both signs, by the two transverse scalar factors, so it is definite
   in some direction.

These cases exhaust the threshold multiplicities on the strict repeated
chart. The independent angular directions (x,y) in each representative
are valid away from the cosine endpoints. Each chosen tangent supplies
a positive derivative after possible reversal; the analytic isolated
full cluster and continuity give the same definiteness in a small full
angular neighborhood. Symmetries transfer the conclusion to the other
strict repeated surfaces. The separately established all-equal and
coordinate-face results concern the other charts. In particular, the
cross-elementary interactions on the open **pairwise-distinct** interior
are a separate next problem; the prior strict-interior no-stationarity
certificate alone only treats each scalar threshold in its own chart.
