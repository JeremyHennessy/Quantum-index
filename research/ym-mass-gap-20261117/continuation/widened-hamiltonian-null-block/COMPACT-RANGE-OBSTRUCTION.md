This is an exact algebraic statement about the truncated quadratic effective operator at fixed lattice spacing, not a conclusion about square-summable responses with tails, embedded eigenvalues, boundary resolvents, spectral density, scattering, finite coupling, or the continuum.

## From arbitrary compact support to a radius-five test

Write J for the actual physical near 129-dimensional space, D for the 348-dimensional flagged detached invariant span, G for the reference-only ghost groups, and A for the separated reference convolution. For any candidate compact solution, average over the eight coordinate-sign reflections; H commutes with them and w is invariant, so an averaged solution would still solve the same nonhomogeneous equation. Extend the physical detached part v by zero on G. The physical near-to-detached coupling is supported entirely on D and the actual detached-to-detached matrix is the corresponding restriction of A. Thus (A−E)v has support only in the union of G, the detached near-output group flags and the actual w_D support. The exact pinned geometry/source radii in the relative anchor convention are respectively 3, 4 and 3. This also holds in the homogeneous problem without averaging if the candidate already lies in the reflection-invariant sector.

The separated 42-by-42 Bloch form M is Laurent, with each individual coordinate exponent in {−1,0,1}. Treat one selected variable z (or its inverse) as the expansion variable and use the field Q(u,v) of rational functions of the untouched variables. Write M = z A_1 + A_0 + z⁻¹ A_−1, using constant-in-z row and column basis changes to place A_1 into an invertible upper-left 34-by-34 block with all other blocks zero. Its Schur complement has an 8-by-8 expansion S_0+z⁻¹S_1+O(z⁻²). If X,Y are right and left leading-kernel bases, S_0 = Yᵀ A_0 X. The exact polynomial-field ranks of A_1 and S_0 are 34 and 6 for all six axis/sign choices. To check that the two-dimensional residual static kernel is lifted at inverse order, use a leading invertible minor A_1[I,J]. The coefficient before projecting into the left/right kernels of S_0 is
```
Yᵀ (A_−1 X − A_0[:,J] A_1[I,J]⁻¹ A_0[I,:] X).
```
At u=3,v=7, each of those two-dimensional tail compressions has full rank modulo 1009 and 1013 (the leading/slab ranks remain exactly 34/6 there). This is a nonvanishing determinant witness over Q(u,v): the pivot and null normalizations used in the specialization lift wherever their nonzero minors are invertible, and the exact generic 34/6 ranks ensure no dimension change. The three pivot degrees are 1 (34), 0 (6), −1 (2), hence det has degree 32, and every matrix element of M⁻¹ has at most degree 1. The argument uses Gaussian block elimination with z-independent initial transformations and off-diagonal correction factors O(z⁻¹) or bounded, so it does not assume an invertible leading face.

A form matrix differs from the operator by an invertible constant Gram, which does not affect the exponent bound. Therefore Mv=q with q supported inside radius four forces every Laurent-polynomial v to have exponent ≤5 in each of the six positive/negative directions. The source q may depend on v and the near variable; the bound uses only its universal support. The reference-slab sign is the inverse of the explicit coefficient-convolution anchor sign, and both signs are verified. The radius is an exact reduction for *all finite supports*, not a presumed truncation.

## Actual equations including exterior clear rows

For a radius bound, keep complete reflection orbits lying inside the cube; all coefficients in a partial orbit must vanish for an invariant candidate. The radius-five cube has 22,170 eligible detached groups, giving 6,420 invariant detached directions, including all 348 old return directions. Add the complete 129 physical near-null variables. The detached propagation uses the original exact 6- and 9-energy stencils, both intermediate directions, the exact 14-dimensional six-loop null projector, and the true shifted E. The physical J↔D cross block and near form are the certified prior computations, with no coupling to unflagged detached shapes. The actual output rows consist of the 477 form rows on D,J followed by every nonzero-reachable exterior raw row at a reflection-orbit representative (13,164 rows, reaching radius six). Reflection invariance means representative raw rows are equivalent to all their translates/reflections within the fibre; no channel is discarded. There are no further possible outputs by the one-step stencil extent. The matrix has 13,641 rows, 6,549 coefficient columns, and 498,441 entries including RHS. Over both primes all rational denominators are invertible, and exact sparse elimination has full coefficient rank 6,549 and augmented rank 6,550. These saturate the corresponding rational (or complex) column upper bounds. Thus the nonhomogeneous compact equation is inconsistent. Separately, the full column rank and same radius bound exclude a nonzero finitely supported **reflection-invariant** homogeneous solution. Averaging need not preserve a non-invariant homogeneous vector, so this certificate does not exclude compact eigenvectors in other reflection sectors.

The smaller calculation restricted inputs to D+J was also exact: 4,392 raw exterior clear rows in 1,686 groups (reach radius five), total 4,869×477, full ranks 477/478 over both primes. This smaller screen alone would not rule out wider compact supports.

## Boundary still open

A Laurent finite-support failure does not prove a nonzero real-shell response. A rational inverse can retain an evanescent factor with no regular zero on the real torus; a noncompact decaying vector could still solve the physical equation. To bridge this algebraic result to spectral weight, one possible separate checkpoint is factor-by-factor coverage of the irreducible determinant factors and their multiplicities by simple real-torus sheets, then local boundary resolvent existence/compatibility at E. The already certified single regular sheet does not provide that factor coverage.
