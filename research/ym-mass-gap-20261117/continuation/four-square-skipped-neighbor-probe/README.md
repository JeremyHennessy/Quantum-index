# A stronger odd-mode probe at the original four-square test coupling

2026-11-27, fixed-spacing open four-square Wilson strip. Use the conventions of the [perimeter-band certificate](../four-square-perimeter-band/README.md). With W_i the half trace of face i, choose the gauge-invariant probe F=W_1 W_3 - W_2 W_4. It is reflection odd and uses two separated faces in each summand; it is not an eigenvector-based observable. By contrast a single face has an [order-four signal](../four-square-odd-quartic/README.md), and a reflection-even probe has exactly zero signal.

At b=0.001 (all spatial couplings equal, temporal link Wilson coefficient 1), the absolute vacuum-to-odd-level matrix element **in unit states** lies between **5.07e-6 and 5.16e-6**. This detects the middle level from the earlier band certificate at the same test coupling. It does not establish the behavior of a single face at this coupling, and it is not a 4D or continuum mass-gap result.

## Leading coefficient

Reference-normalize the generalized eigenvectors u=M psi by their free components (vacuum v and odd o=(124-134)/sqrt(2)). The transition is u_o^T F exp(-bV) u_v. Taking the first derivative distributes the one spatial insertion over the two vectors and the exponential. Parity leaves only target 134 for W_1 W_3 and target 124 for W_2 W_4. The binary loop rule and the single resolvent terms give the exact derivative sqrt(2)*a^2/[16*(1-a^4)] with a=I_2(1)/I_1(1). The verifier uses exact symbolic algebra. The full function is odd in b: simultaneous flip of all face holonomies sends V to -V, leaves F fixed, changes the free odd vector sign, and fixes the vacuum.

## Fifth-order enclosure and tail

The [physical spin-network construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9906036) and earlier exact recoupling code apply. A direct term through degree five contains up to seven face hits, with two probe factors. Path pruning uses the same prefix/suffix parity-adjusted bound as the quartic check. Of 16,384 seven-hit words, 2,016 end in each required parity boundary. Every cut satisfies twice-spin <=3 and weight <=18. Shorter words can be padded by two equal adjacent hits at the vacuum, so their cap is also covered. For eigenvalue counterterms up to order four, the intermediate walks start and end at the vacuum or binary odd pivots of weight 10. On either half of a closed scalar walk there are at most two hits, so the intermediate maximum twice-spin is 3 and weight at most 18. Their lower-degree partner terms are already covered; no approximation is introduced by omitting labels outside the cap.

The interval replay checks each recoupling entry against its transpose, preserves the eigenvalue counterterms, and uses positive Bessel-series enclosures and 60-digit interval arithmetic. Leading coefficients (rounded here solely for readability) are 0.005116421080894, -0.002789804226252 and 0.000806586600852 at degrees one, three and five. The two reflected products are independently evaluated.

For the complex circle of relative radius .16 about each free pivot, the spectral projection is simple and analytic for |b|<=.02 by the same Neumann inverse as in the quartic check. The complement of the respective pivot in its reflection sector is at least .16 times the free eigenvalue away from any z inside that circle (including the interacting eigenvalue). Thus |z| times the complementary free resolvent norm is <=7.25. With rho=4|b| and alpha=7.25*rho/(1-rho), the complementary block of K-z exp(-bV) is invertible for alpha<1. A nonzero eigenvector must have nonzero reference component. In reference normalization its perpendicular component is bounded by alpha/(1-alpha), by the complementary equation. Consequently each generalized vector has norm <2 on |b|=.02. Since ||F||<=2 and ||exp(-bV)||<=1/(1-rho), the analytic transition there has modulus <18. This tail bound is for reference-normalized vectors, not Riesz-projected vectors with a different normalization.

Oddness bounds the tail after degree five by 18*(b/.02)^7/(1-(b/.02)^2), below 1.41e-8 at b=.001. The reference-normalized transition lies in (5.1023e-6,5.1306e-6). For real b each physical norm ||M^-1 u|| is between 1-2b and sqrt(1+(alpha/(1-alpha))^2)/(1-2b), which gives the claimed unit-state interval. No numerical eigenvector residual is used.
