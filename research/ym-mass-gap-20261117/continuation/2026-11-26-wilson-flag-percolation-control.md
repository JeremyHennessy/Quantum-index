# A percolating SU(2) flag control at small bare Wilson coupling

**2026-11-26 UTC. Exact obstruction to inferring rare connected flags from finite energy or a local core repair. Not the continuum trajectory or a statement about forced-address connectivity.**

The [local repair](2026-11-26-wilson-local-flag-repair.md) supplies positive conditional probability of a good bounded core. That property, even with ordinary Wilson plaquette geometry, does not imply that connected bad flags are typically bounded. The following example uses the four-dimensional fundamental SU(2) Wilson law itself, but at a small fixed bare coupling, not the running coupling or its blocked law.

## An embedded independent or dominated flag process

In a coordinate plane of the four-dimensional lattice write z=(x_1,x_2,0,0). At each z select the plaquette based at z along directions 3 and 4. Selected plaquettes for different z share no links. Declare z open if that plaquette's chord exceeds a fixed threshold t≤1; every open z is flagged by the exact [Wilson flag definition](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG/Masks.lean#L26-L37).

At beta=0 the link law is independent Haar, plaquette products are Haar, and the selected open indicators are independent. The SU(2) Haar cap calculation at chord 1 gives h(1)=1/3-sqrt(3)/(4 pi), so their open probability is at least p0=2/3+sqrt(3)/(4 pi)=0.80449889... . Under the sup-distance-one graph used for [support connectivity](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Lattice/Support.lean#L39-L64), the plane contains the triangular graph generated by (1,0), (0,1), and (1,1), with negatives. Independent site percolation on that graph is supercritical at p0, since its critical probability is 1/2 ([Smirnov–Werner discussion with attribution](https://www.unige.ch/~smirnov/papers/smw-j.pdf)). In particular the origin has positive probability to lie in an infinite connected component of flags.

The phenomenon persists for a quantitative interval of positive beta. Choose one distinguished link of each selected plaquette. Given all other links, the conditional density of the distinguished link relative to probability Haar is at least exp(-12 beta), since exactly six plaquettes can see the link and each Wilson price is in [0,2]. This uses the [Wilson energy normalization](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/PublicWilson.lean#L17-L29). Haar variation of that link makes the selected plaquette holonomy Haar, by invariance under left and right multiplication and inversion. Hence the conditional open probability is at least p_beta=p0 exp(-12 beta). In any ordered list of distinct selected plaquettes, previous open indicators are measurable in the other links for the next distinguished link. By the tower property this same lower bound holds conditional on all previous indicators. Coupling the indicators sequentially with iid uniforms proves stochastic domination of iid Bernoulli(p_beta) on every finite subset; this is not an assumption of Wilson independence. The assertion holds first on sufficiently large finite tori, passes to thermodynamic subsequential limits on the finite selected-indicator laws (their threshold boundaries are Haar-null, and fixed finite-link conditional densities are bounded above as well), and extends to the origin-to-distance events. Thus flags percolate with positive origin probability whenever
\[
 0\le\beta<\frac{\log(2p_0)}{12}=0.03963429\ldots .
\]

## Scope and consequence

This rules out a general inference from local finite energy, chord thresholds, and bounded good-core replacement to an exponential tail for nearest-neighbor flag-cluster radius. Even the independent-link case has percolating flags at these thresholds. It does not rule out small flags under the **actual blocked distribution at a specific physical level**. A connected component in the eligible-address overlap graph is also not the geometric flag cluster: flags can be connected while supports factor, or a long support can bridge isolated flags. The forced trace argument therefore needs a quantitative, measure-specific control of cross-buffer address contributions relative to the covering sum, rather than a generic assertion that good local core events imply small clusters. No decay or spectral conclusion follows.
