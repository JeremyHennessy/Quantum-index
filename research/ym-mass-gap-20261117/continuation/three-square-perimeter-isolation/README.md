# Three-square Wilson strip: separate the perimeter from the disjoint end faces

November 27, 2026 UTC. Continuation of the [two-square overlap certificate](../two-square-overlap-certificate/README.md). This is an exact finite, open three-square spatial calculation. It deliberately distinguishes the isotropic-temporal problem from a shared-edge anisotropic control. It does not give a four-dimensional or infinite-volume result.

## The two directions are degenerate before the spatial perturbation

Use the [physical spin-network basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9906036) and the [positive Wilson transfer](https://bib-pubdb1.desy.de/record/396349). The two exterior three-link paths have labels L,R; the shared left and right vertical links have labels J,K; the middle top and bottom links have labels T,U. At the four trivalent vertices the triangles are (L,J,T), (L,J,U), (R,K,T), (R,K,U), with multiplicity exactly one for admissible even-sum triangles. Bivalent vertices preserve the representation and all three temporal link factors on each exterior path. For temporal coefficient β=1 on every link the kinetic eigenvalue is a_L³a_R³a_Ja_Ka_Ta_U, where a_n=I_(n+1)(1)/I_1(1), and an edge with label n contributes a_n≤4^(−n). Group by total weighted label 3L+3R+J+K+T+U. Exact low-weight counts are 1,3,2,7 at weights 0,4,6,8 and zero at other weights up to 8. The only all-binary weight-8 states are the perimeter (1,1,0,0,1,1) and the disjoint end faces (1,1,1,1,0,0). Both have multiplier a₁⁸.

Put V=Σ_i b_i W_i, |b_i|≤1/100, and S=exp(V/2) K exp(V/2). Its eigenvalues are between e^(−.03) and e^.03 times the corresponding ranked kinetic eigenvalues by the min–max comparison from the two-square calculation. These bounds concern the **unnormalized** S with temporally normalized K; no division by the interacting vacuum eigenvalue is made here. Exact rational Bessel bounds .240<a₁<.241, a₂<.040 give an isolated rank-two band inside [1.0676×10⁻⁵,1.1733×10⁻⁵]. Lower spectrum is <8.081×10⁻⁶ and higher spectrum >1.85×10⁻⁴. At n≥9 use 4⁻⁹; for a nonbinary weight-8 state replace at least one pair of fundamental factors by a factor at most .040. This identifies a band, not a perimeter-specific eigenvector or overlap at nonzero b.

## Resolve the ambiguity in a controlled anisotropic test

Set β=.8 **only on the two shared vertical links**; put β=1 elsewhere and leave the b_i unchanged. The Bessel multiplier c₁ on either shared link satisfies .19<c₁<.20, and its next multiplier is <.040. The perimeter still has d=a₁⁸, but the disjoint pair has a₁⁶ c₁². It drops below the same 8.081×10⁻⁶ upper bound after the spatial perturbation. The higher states (each single face and each neighboring two-face perimeter, and the vacuum) are above 1.46×10⁻⁴, using the exact low-weight labels and c₁>.19. Thus the same stated band now contains **just one** eigenvalue, continuously connected to the perimeter. The time convention still refers to actual Wilson links, not a diagonal projection imposed by hand.

## A perimeter probe sees it at b_i=1/100

There is a certified nonzero overlap for the perimeter half-trace F_o. This is not yet the sought *single local face* overlap. Here is a dimension-free resolvent check for this fixed geometry, avoiding a spectral truncation. Let P be the interacting isolated perimeter projector, P_ref the projector onto its normalized kinetic reference e_o. Other kinetic eigenvalues lie below 3d/4 or above 2d, by the exact bounds above. On the circle |w−d|=d/8 the bounds are ‖(K−w)⁻¹‖≤8/d and ‖(K−w)⁻¹K‖≤8 (verify separately at d, below 3d/4, and above 2d). Use D=K exp(V), similar to S by M=exp(V/2). The resolvent expansion for D is controlled by δ=8(e^.03−1)<.248. Integrating the difference on the circle gives ‖P_D−P_ref‖≤δ/(1−δ). Conjugating back adds at most e^.03−1 and multiplies that norm by at most e^.03. Hence ‖P−P_ref‖<.38. The contour keeps exactly its one-dimensional range along sV for 0≤s≤1.

The actual unit vacuum Ω can be phased so ‖Ω−1‖<.05. In detail K outside the constant state is ≤4⁻⁴, λ0(S)≥1 by Jensen (each W_i has zero Haar mean), and ‖S−K‖≤e^.03−1<.031. The projected eigenvalue equation and the phase choice give the upper bound √2(.031)/(1−4⁻⁴)<.05. If ψ is the unit continued perimeter state, its overlap with e_o has magnitude ≥√(1−.38²)>.9. The exact Wilson perimeter character is e_o, so F_o·1=e_o/2 and ‖F_o‖≤1. Thus |⟨ψ,F_o Ω⟩|>.9/2−.05=.4. Exact vacuum subtraction does not change this transition.

## Remaining single-face test

A separate center flip on a nonshared edge of each face makes the perimeter signature 111, the end-pair signature 101, and the left-face insertion signature 100. Thus a left-face overlap with a continued perimeter direction is odd separately in b₂,b₃ and even in b₁; the end-pair direction is allowed already at b₃. These are selection rules, not a proof that the perimeter coefficient is nonzero. At isotropic temporal coupling, total band projection cannot distinguish these effects. The anisotropic control now supplies a simple target for a separate local-correlation certificate, with the perimeter observable as a positive control.
