[Calendar.Fits.logCost and deep](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG/History.lean#L91-L119) give log columns(m+1)≤log L_m / J(m+1)^2, with J(m+1)→infinity. The sum of log L_m equals the difference of endpoint log inverse meshes, so any epsilon bounds the late part by epsilon times log(1/a_N); the early part is constant. Thus this bound on the terminal inverse is subpower in 1/a_N, uniformly over periods sharing these bounds. This does not presume that the terminal transfer is close to the identity.

The [diagonal scaling](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Labels/Reference.lean#L58-L67) costs at most a fixed power of sqrt(hatBeta_N): the threshold is at least hatBeta_N to the power −1/2 and all exponents are fixed in a finite frame. HatBeta is linear in log inverse mesh. Each fixed section basis polynomial is bounded on its compact link stencil. Together with the explicit inverse fourth mesh power in initial, this gives ||O_N||_infinity ≤ a_N^(−4−o(1)) for the cylinder at a fixed reference with bounded target coefficients. This is only an upper bound, not a statement about its typical size or its continuum norm.

At each fine anchor x, omit O_N(x) on an event B_x requiring r_bad distinct internal plaquettes of one fixed tile to have price at least t. On tori satisfying [the exact tiling hypotheses](2026-11-26-wilson-exterior-chessboard-weight.md), the centered omission after conditioning on any sigma field of the full joint experiment has L² norm at most ||O_N||_infinity sqrt(P_bare(B_x)). Centering reduces norm, conditional expectation is an L² contraction, and the fine marginal of the joint experiment remains the bare law. This includes conditioning on the entire noisy coarse output, but does not establish pointwise posterior rarity. For a compact smearing with weights a_N^4 f(a_N x) and bounded sum of absolute weights, the triangle inequality yields the same rate up to a constant, without paying the number of cells. Two-packet time differences preserve the rate.

With kappa a positive lower limit of beta_N/log(1/a_N), this error vanishes in absolute L² for r_bad t kappa>8. Any desired fixed r_bad fits in a large enough fixed tile, whose entropy cost stays subpower. This verifies the coefficient scaling required for omitting sufficiently large fixed high-curvature patterns on applicable periods. It does not show that the pinned periods admit those tilings, dispose of isolated bad plaquettes, bound the fraction of a vanishing signal, or control typical exteriors. Comparing two correlations needs also a partner norm; using only the same crude sup bound for that partner instead requires r_bad t kappa>16. No assumption is made about coincident-point continuum trace L².
