## Construct the token once

Write P for the grade-four quotient, A for the raw signed time reflection, and J for raw conjugation (conjugate-linear). Let Θ=TraceExtension.center F, and choose v with P v=Θ on the already chosen hierarchy. The raw involutions satisfy A²=J²=1 and AJ=JA. Define
\[
   v_{\mathrm{sym}}=\tfrac14(v+Av+Jv+AJv).
\]
The coefficients are real, so A and J both fix v_sym. Each term projects to Θ by source chain 1–2, hence P v_sym=Θ. For mere block adjoint matching the smaller average (v+AJv)/2 suffices; it need not be real and time-even separately. For example, with one physical real-even coordinate and a null imaginary time-odd coordinate, AJ fixes both but the four-term average removes the latter. This is a linear-algebra control only.

The resulting grade-four token is chosen once, independently of cutoff and volume, and its insertion is gauge invariant and local by the same public witness. It is not a fixed cutoff replacement equal pointwise to every other lift; only its projected field and **ordered separated limits** coincide. No raw null insertion is presumed to vanish at finite cutoff.

For N≥ref(4), the native pointwise identities now say (i) O(v_sym,x;U) is real and (ii) O(v_sym,x;pull(R,b)U)=O(v_sym,affine(R,b)x;U). Orientation of individual reflected links is already part of pull; no plaquette approximation or discarded contact term is involved. With the same real test f and position convention as the earlier packet, a lattice reflection maps the packet to its exact reflected-weight packet; lattice translation moves it by grid-exact physical delays. For fixed compact separated supports, large enough periods avoid wrap at the coordinate chart seam, and sufficiently small mesh keeps the fixed stencil away from the slab faces. The stencil is used for support, not to infer equality.

Consequently the sewn finite-period pairing uses **the same** v_sym token on the two sides and matches the ordinary public two-token smear, not a new unlabeled correlation. ## What changed, and what has not

The fixed-token correspondence needed for the periodic test is now discharged by a concrete representative and verified source identities. It is no longer the first obstacle. The outstanding question is a bound on the normalized local trace-commutator pairing, with strictly positive exponential rate in **physical time** and the running bare coupling of the same Wilson family. A finite transfer eigenvalue, positivity of curvature, nonzero leading short-distance amplitude, or a thermodynamic estimate at fixed bare coupling does not supply that bound. The construction neither computes Wilson moments nor proves decay, a trace-sector gap, the full Hamiltonian gap, or novelty relative to the literature.
