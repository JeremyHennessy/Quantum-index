# Forced trace response: exact cancellation of disconnected flag components

**2026-11-26 UTC. Finite, configuration-by-configuration algebra for the pinned blocked trace. No uniform infrared estimate.**

The [blocked-score identity](2026-11-26-wilson-trace-blocked-score.md) reduces the forced part of the normalized insertion to the marked forced sum divided by the unmarked gas. The explicit forced-address rules let us cancel disconnected components exactly, including for signed activities. This removes one spurious global denominator, but not the local one.

## Exact reduction at any positive-density configuration

Fix cutoff, level, period, configuration, and a single anchored insertion. In the pinned [forced format](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG/Activities.lean#L38-L76), each eligible address has a nonempty inventory contained in its support and in the configuration's finite flag set F. A collection is admissible when its supports are pairwise disjoint and its inventories cover F exactly ([gas definition](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/RG/Activities.lean#L102-L118)). Ineligible addresses can be dropped from all nonzero products, in either vacuum or marked sum, by the source eligibility of both weights. Use **all** eligible addresses in the graph, not just those with nonzero vacuum weight, since the pinned source allows a source-only address.

Join two eligible addresses when their supports intersect. Any flag occurring in two inventories connects their vertices, so it belongs to just one component. Every component owns at least one flag, so there are finitely many components even if its address set is countable. If a flag is unowned then the gas vanishes; at positive density this cannot occur. For a component C let G_C be its signed vacuum covering sum for its own flags. Global admissible eligible covers consist exactly of one such cover per component. By the pinned [absolute summability bounds](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Source/Preservation.lean#L22-L47), rearranging is legitimate. Therefore the full gas is the finite product of G_C.

Every nonzero forced mark has support containing the insertion anchor ([marked format](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-YM-20261028/blob/3456e5e75cf8d4cd558955a5c7638fba2d68f464/YM/Source/Insertion.lean#L37-L54)). Those addresses all lie in one component C_o because they intersect at that anchor, and a disjoint cover can contain at most one marked address. If no forced marks are nonzero then the forced response is zero. Otherwise let M_Co be the sum with one marked factor in that component. Global forced sum equals M_Co times the product of all other G_C. The gas is positive wherever the actual coarse density is positive, so no component in that product is zero. Hence the exact forced ratio is M_Co/G_Co (and the blocked physical insertion also carries its explicit inverse mesh factor). There is no need for a lower bound on the product over disconnected components or for nonnegative polymer weights. This is a fixed-configuration algebraic reduction: the rooted component may be large or depend on distant flags.

## The rooted lower-bound problem is real

Exact one-flag control: two addresses are eligible and overlap, both with the same one-flag inventory. Let their unmarked weights be b and (epsilon-b), with 0<epsilon<b; mark only the first with weight delta. The admissible covers are precisely the two singletons, so the gas is positive epsilon but the ratio is delta/epsilon. Fix any positive absolute budget and choose b and delta below it, then let epsilon shrink. Even nonnegative weights (epsilon,0) give the same issue with a source-only or small vacuum weight. Adding an independent flag with a vacuum weight r>0 changes both numerator and denominator by r and leaves the ratio unchanged. These are activity-algebra controls, not realizations of the exact Wilson trajectory or counterexamples to a Wilson estimate.

The standard small-activity expansion for a gas with an available empty cover cannot be imported solely on the absolute weights here: with F nonempty the empty global cover is prohibited. The unflagged case is different, and the source already proves gas=1 there. The remaining target is now local in this specific sense: bound the rooted quotient in squared mean under the true blocked law, controlling component size and marked weight **relative to the rooted covering sum**. The result does not remove the separate two-mark conditional covariance, prove screening, or constrain the continuum mass gap.
