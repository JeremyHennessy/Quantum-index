# Source-qualified completion campaign, 21 October 2026

This is a bounded scientific-content release, not a claim to have completed the theories themselves. The original release records and scientific file prefixes remain intact.

## Qualified deliverables

- Eighteen model-specific first mathematical representatives cover the twelve remaining astroparticle/cosmology gaps and six quantum-cosmology gaps identified after PR86. Every equation has source-version/page/equation locators, units, assumptions and explicit limits. See the mechanism-limits inventory for what each representative does not establish.
- Six cosmology Passports connect existing formulas, assumptions, evidence and open Problems. Four perturbation-formula records gain source-reviewed metadata without changing their equations. The preheating source-section locator is corrected to Section VI, not IV.
- Five dated empirical records distinguish Planck inflation constraints, BK18 tensor bounds, primordial deuterium, KATRIN effective electron-neutrino mass and a model-dependent Planck+BAO neutrino mass-sum bound. They are not presented as timeless universal bounds, or as interchangeable observables. Existing DESI and dark-matter records are linked rather than duplicated.
- Four scientific Problems describe inflationary mechanisms, baryogenesis, expansion-history tensions and quantum-to-classical primordial statistics. None is marked solved.
- EFT of inflation and transient scalar early dark energy are distinct additions. Historical generic early dark energy, perturbation/transfer frameworks and later non-Gaussianity/collider extensions have explicit nonduplicate dispositions.

## Scientific review and limits

The mathematical expressions and conventions were checked independently against primary papers or the stated authoritative review. Important checks include single-field reduced versus unreduced Planck conventions, canonical versus phantom stress-energy signs, local fixed-density SIDM normalization, the distinction between sterile-neutrino mixing and production, the background-specific matter-contraction mode equation, Riemannian spinfoam scope and the explicitly schematic/constraint-incomplete third-quantization free action. Third quantization is not presented as a completed quantum-gravity theory.

The empirical records retain analysis dates, dataset combinations, confidence levels, model assumptions and nonclaims. Cosmological fits do not directly identify an inflaton, dark-matter particle, early-dark-energy field, or the physical mechanism of decoherence.

## Regression controls

The campaign ledger records every added or modified runtime record and reconstructs the complete pre-campaign records exactly. Original files remain byte-identical prefixes. Only dated historical-checkpoint suites read a SHA-256-gated pre-campaign projection; generic schema, reference, freshness, catalog and navigation tests use the full current runtime. Mutation tests reject omitted or altered records. Browser tests render every campaign formula through MathJax, check source links and phone containment, and exercise all six Passports at desktop and phone widths.

Scientific review, passing local tests and a published branch are separate gates. Exact-head CI, merged-main CI and served deployment verification must be recorded before a production completion claim.
