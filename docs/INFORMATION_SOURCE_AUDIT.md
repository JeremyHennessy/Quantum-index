# Information and entanglement source audit

Nine existing formulas now have explicit source-located assumptions and convention notes. No equations, identities, formula IDs, theory links or coarse formula types changed. This is curation, not a new derivation, empirical result or discovery.

The Watrous reference is the author-hosted 2018 pre-publication textbook draft. Negativity conventions were checked against Vidal and Werner's arXiv v1 manuscript dated 22 February 2001, associated with the 2002 journal paper. Exact printed/PDF pages and equation numbers appear on the formula cards.

- Relative entropy requires support inclusion for a finite value and otherwise extends to positive infinity. Logarithm bases distinguish bits from nats.
- Mutual information includes classical and quantum correlations. The Holevo quantity bounds accessible information but is not generally attained.
- Coherent information can be negative and is not, by itself, regularized channel capacity.
- Strong subadditivity and data processing are inequalities, despite the catalog's retained coarse historical formula-type label.
- Negativity and logarithmic negativity require the stated partial-transpose conventions. A zero value on a PPT state does not establish separability.
- The displayed state-only diamond-norm optimization is qualified to finite-dimensional Hermiticity-preserving maps with an input-sized reference. It is not the unrestricted arbitrary-map definition; the norm is unhalved.

All nine records were independently source-reviewed before integration.
