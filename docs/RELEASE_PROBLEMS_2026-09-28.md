- low-energy gravitational EFT is distinguished from proposed UV completions;
- ETH, integrability, MBL and scars are separated by regime and mechanism;
- DESI is background cosmology context on the Dark Matter page, not a direct dark-matter detection;
- the 2026 LZ excess remains a 2.6σ global candidate signal, not a discovery.
